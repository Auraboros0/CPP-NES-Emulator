CPU: 8-bit 2A03 (6502 Core)
     1.79MHz clock (In NTSC version)
Graphics: 2C02 PPU
RAM: 2KB
VRAM: 2KB
5 sound channels

The color of a pixel is represented by 2 bits, meaning every sprite has 4 colors to choose from (Anything other than tiles usually reserve one for transparency). The specific colors are determined by the PPU
